# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Kova is a small project with contributors and users from a lot of different
backgrounds. We want issues, PRs, and the [Matrix
room](https://matrix.to/#/#kova-md:matrix.org) to stay a place where anyone
can ask a question or propose a change without being made to feel unwelcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful in disagreements — critique the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, especially in text where tone is easy to misread.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a PR or proposal; a "no" isn't an
  invitation to escalate.
- Keep discussion on-topic in issues and PRs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in DMs.
- Discriminatory jokes or language related to gender, sexual orientation,
  disability, race, religion, or similar.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, PRs, or the Matrix room.

## Scope

This applies to GitHub (issues, PRs, discussions), the Matrix room, and any
other space where you're representing yourself as part of the Kova project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to [ross@kova.md](mailto:ross@kova.md),
or ping a maintainer directly in the Matrix room if you'd rather not email.
Reports are handled in confidence — we won't share who raised it without
permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will respond proportionately: a private word, a warning, or
removal from the project's spaces (GitHub org, Matrix room) for repeated or
serious violations. Deliberate abuse of the reporting process is itself a
violation.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), trimmed to fit a small
project.
